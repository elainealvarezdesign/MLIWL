    After you watch

    Easy ways to start talking

    • “What did you think of Kyle’s choices?”

      Talking about a character feels safer than talking about yourself.

    • “Has anyone you know felt like that?”

      Gives them room to share a friend’s story — or their own.

    • “I’m glad you told me. How can I help?”

      If they open up, listen first. You don’t need to fix it right away.

    Know the signs

    When to get help

    If you notice any of these, talk to your teen and reach out for support. If they’re in danger, call 911.

    Call or text 988 See all help lines
    • Talking about wanting to die or being a burden
    • Pulling away from friends, family or activities
    • Big changes in sleep, eating or mood
    • Giving away things they care about
    • Using more alcohol or drugs
    • Searching online for ways to hurt themselves
