About the series

Stories that remind teens their life is worth living

My Life Is Worth Living is a free animated series about teen mental health. In each story, a relatable teen faces something hard — and learns they don’t have to face it alone.

The idea

No one gets through it alone

Each character wrestles with thoughts that feel like they’re working against them. Over the course of their story, they learn that support can come from family, friends, or people they least expect — and that sharing a burden makes it lighter.

Life can seem bleak, and negative thoughts are often part of the experience. But no situation is hopeless, everyone deserves love and support, and every life is worth living.

Why it matters

Connection can be the difference

Research shows that connection to friends, family and community protects young people. Stories break the silence and make it easier to ask for help.
