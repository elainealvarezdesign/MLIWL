    Safe viewing

    Before you show a story

    These stories deal with real topics like self-harm and substance use. A few minutes of prep helps every student feel safe.

    Download the safe-viewing guide

    • Read the content note

      Each story lists its topics. Let students know what to expect and that they can step out.

    • Have support ready

      Tell your school counselor which story you’re showing and when.

    • If a student opens up

      Listen, thank them, and follow your school’s referral process. Share 988 if there’s risk.
