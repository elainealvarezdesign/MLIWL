    About the series

    Built on the power of human connection

    My Life Is Worth Living™ is the first animated series that models the human connection shown to be protective against suicide. Each story follows a teen facing one of the hardest things young people deal with — and shows their path to the key decision: that life is worth living.

    Videos available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese and Mandarin.
