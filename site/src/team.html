Founders

The people behind the series

  • GJ

    Greg and Julie Cook

    Founders, The Cook Center for Human Connection

    Greg and Julie founded the Cook Center in 2020 and fund most of the series. Suicide prevention became one of their family foundation’s pillars after losses that marked them both deeply.

  • TT

    Terry Thoren

    CEO, Wonder Media

    Terry is the driving force behind the series. He left Hollywood to launch Wonder Media after leading Klasky Csupo, the studio behind Rugrats and The Wild Thornberrys.
