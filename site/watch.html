    Need to talk to someone now?

    Reaching out is a sign of strength. These services are free, confidential and open 24/7.

    • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

      Call or text 988, or chat online. Free, confidential, 24/7.

    • Crisis Text Line

      Text HOME to 741741 to reach a trained crisis counselor.
