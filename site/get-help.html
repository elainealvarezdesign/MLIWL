For friends, parents and teachers

Worried about someone?

You don’t need the perfect words. Asking directly about suicide does not put the idea in someone’s head — it shows you care.

Read the parent playbooks
  1. 1

    Ask

    “Are you thinking about suicide?” Ask calmly and directly.

  2. 2

    Listen

    Let them talk. Don’t judge, argue or promise to keep it secret.

  3. 3

    Stay with them

    If they’re in danger, don’t leave them alone. Remove things they could use to hurt themselves.

  4. 4

    Connect them to help

    Call or text 988 together, or call 911 in an emergency.
